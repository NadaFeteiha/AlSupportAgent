# Bayfront Grand Hotel — Guest Policy & Amenity Reference

## Room Types

### Standard Room
Rate: $159/night
Occupancy: 2 guests
Amenities: Queen bed, city view, free WiFi, work desk
Cancellation: Free up to 48 hours before check-in

### Deluxe Room
Rate: $199/night
Occupancy: 2-3 guests
Amenities: King bed, partial ocean view, free WiFi, mini bar, balcony
Cancellation: Free up to 48 hours before check-in

### Ocean View Suite
Rate: $289/night
Occupancy: 4 guests
Amenities: King bed, separate living area, full ocean view, free WiFi,
mini bar, balcony, complimentary breakfast
Cancellation: Free up to 72 hours before check-in

## Cancellation and Refund Policy

### Cancellation Windows
- Standard and Deluxe rooms: free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in
- Ocean View Suite: free cancellation up to 72 hours before check-in
- Late cancellations are charged one night's rate as a fee
- No-shows are charged the full reservation total

### Refund Timeline
- Credit or Debit Card: 3-5 business days
- Hotel Gift Card: 2-3 hours
- Bank Account via ACH: up to 10 business days

### How to Cancel a Reservation
1. Go to My Reservations in your guest account
2. Select the reservation you want to cancel
3. Confirm the cancellation and choose a refund method

## Guest Rewards Program

### Tiers
- Silver: 0 to 999 points
- Gold: 1000 to 4999 points
- Platinum: 5000 or more points

### Earning Points
- Standard and Deluxe rooms: 1 point per $1 spent
- Ocean View Suite and other premium rooms: 2 points per $1 spent
- Dining and spa charges: 3 points per $1 spent

### Redeeming Points
- 100 points equals $1 of stay credit
- Minimum redemption is 500 points
- Points are valid for 2 years from the date earned

### Tier Benefits
- Silver: early check-in when available
- Gold: free room upgrade when available, 10% discount on stays
- Platinum: guaranteed late checkout, free breakfast, 15% discount on stays,
  priority guest support

## Amenities

- Free WiFi throughout the property
- Rooftop pool and fitness center, open 6am-10pm
- On-site restaurant and bar
- Valet parking available for $25/night
- Pet-friendly rooms available on request (additional cleaning fee applies)
